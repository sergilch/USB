afe_receiver_tr schematic

Transistor-level design of the HS receive front end that afe_receiver.va models: HS receiver with hysteresis, squelch detector with its envelope filter, HS disconnect detector, UVLO and enable. 180 nm: 3.3 V thick-oxide input stages on RX_VDD, 1.8 V core devices for everything else on RX_VDDO. Simulated with ngspice.

Open points

  • Monte Carlo: preamp and comparator offsets go straight into the squelch and disconnect thresholds (preamp gain about 1). The squelch margin is 8–10 mV at tt but only 4.5 / 5 mV at ss; trims SQ_VTH / DISC_VTH / RX_HYST map onto RR1–RR3 and the XHS cross-coupled width.
  • HS RX delay 0.9 ns (1.24 ns at ss): rerun the A3 squelch / data alignment of spec 4.2 with it, or add a faster second stage.
  • Squelch with the USB eye and a cable model, not only alternating data with linear edges.
  • Power 4.7 mW: the squelch preamps (400 µA at 3.3 V) are sized for 0.1 ns recovery.
  • Turn-on time (about 170 ns) needs a figure in the interface spec.
  • Noise, ESD, xh018 models: not modelled yet; rerun all corners on the PDK.
