afe_pll_tr schematic

Transistor-level design of the 480 MHz PLL that afe_pll.va models: TSPC phase-frequency detector, 20 µA current-steering charge pump, R2 / C3 / C4 filter, the CDR's VCO cell unchanged, and a ÷20 / ÷25 divider selected by REFSEL. 180 nm, 1.8 V core devices, simulated with ngspice.

Why the slow corner fails: the shared VCO's coarse curve compresses at 1.62 V, and the fine reference VDD/2 drops to 0.81 V, so the loop needs about 1.38 V on CDR_CH_OUT_PLL, while the charge pump's PMOS side runs out of headroom at about 1.36 V. The fix belongs in the shared VCO cell (more range at low VDD, or a VDD-independent fine reference for both VCOs), together with the xh018 models.

Open points

  • Lock fails at ss / 125 °C / 1.62 V. Add VCO range at low VDD in the shared cell, or make the fine references (PLL vref and the CDR reset divider together) independent of VDD, or supply the VCO from a regulator.
  • PLL–CDR VCO mismatch (R3): +0.4 % systematic from the two fine references; derive both from one divider, then Monte Carlo with xh018 mismatch models.
  • Noise: jitter, reference spurs and supply sensitivity are not simulated.
  • Lock detector in the loop: the 5120 ± 5 window count is checked only by the Spectre testbench tb_afe_pll_tr.scs (written, not run).
  • xh018 models: replace generic180, use the PDK resistors and capacitors, retune the shared VCO, rerun both testbenches.
