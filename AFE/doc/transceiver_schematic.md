# Transceiver schematic — `afe_transceiver_tr` (`AFE/spice/afe_transceiver_tr.sp`)

*Transistor-level design of the USB 2.0 line transceiver that `AFE/veriloga/afe_transceiver.va` models ([transceiver.md](transceiver.md)): HS current-mode driver, FS / LS drivers, the three FS / LS receivers, the 1.5 kΩ pull-up, the 45 Ω HS terminations and the 15 kΩ pull-downs, same pins, same off states. Process: 180 nm, 1.8 V core devices plus 3.3 V thick-oxide devices for everything on the line, sized and simulated with the placeholder `AFE/spice/models/generic180.lib` (see [AFE/spice/README.md](../spice/README.md)). Status: 2026-11-14, simulated with ngspice 42 at tt, ss / 125 °C / 3.0 / 1.62 V and ff / −40 °C / 3.6 / 1.98 V (section 4): tt and ff pass; ss fails one testbench tolerance, the HS delay of 1.34 ns against the corner window of 1.3 ns (no USB limit, open point 8); not run in Spectre; no layout; no Monte Carlo.*

The same schematic as a page with the drawn sheets and result tables: [transceiver_schematic.html](transceiver_schematic.html) (open it in a browser; a snapshot of 2026-11-14, not regenerated when the netlist changes).

The netlist is the source of the schematic; device names below are its instance names (sizes W/L in µm; "33" = thick-oxide `pch33` / `nch33`, L ≥ 0.35 µm; "m×" = multiplier). Drawn sheets: `AFE/spice/doc/xc_{top,hs,pre,bias,fs,rx,term}.svg`, generated by `AFE/spice/doc/draw_transceiver.py`. 318 MOSFET instances (152 core, 166 thick oxide).

## 1. Block diagram

```
          VDD18 (1.8 V, core devices)                       VDD33 (3.3 V, thick oxide)
 TX_IN_P ─┬─ XI0..XI3 ─ OAI21 XGP/XGN ─ XBP/XBN ─ TG ─► gp, gn ─┐
 TX_IN_N ─┘        rdy ─┘           ready ─ XBD ─ TG ─► gd ─────┤   XS 356×10/1 (vbp) ─ XE (e33b) ─ cs
 TX_EN ─ & pok ─ ten ─ ready timer CR (16 ns) ─► rdy          └─► XSP → DP · XSN → DM · XSD → RDQ 22.5 Ω
             └─ ls_up ─► e33 / e33b (XE on, parking off) ;  CK 1 pF: eb2 → vbp (turn-on kick)
 FS_OE/DAT/SE0/LS ─ XF0..XF14 & pok ─ ls_up ×5 ─► fs_drv XFP (DP), XFM (DM): push-pull + RS 26 Ω, Miller slew
 RXDP_A, RXDM_A ◄─ & on ─ ls_dn ◄─ se_rx Schmitt (1.26 / 1.60 V) ◄── DP, DM
 RXRCV_A        ◄─ | !on ─ ls_dn ◄─ diff_rx rail-to-rail, hysteresis ◄── DP − DM
 DP_PULLUP_EN_A, HS_TERM_EN_A, PD_EN_A0/1 ─ ls_up ─► XPU + 1.405 kΩ · XHP/XHM + 41.6 Ω · XP0/XP1 + 14 kΩ
 TX_VREF ─► xc_bias: OTA + replica, I = V(TX_VREF) / 12 kΩ = 100 µA ─► vbp, nb ;  uvlo: VDD33 > 2.94 V ─► pok
```

Two supply domains, as in the model. **VDD33** carries everything that touches DP / DM: the HS current source and its switches, the FS / LS output stages, the terminations, the receivers, the bias and the UVLO, all thick oxide. **VDD18** carries the input logic, the HS pre-drivers, the ready timer and the receiver output gating: every output swings 0 … VDD18, every input is a 1.8 V logic level. The 480 Mbit/s path crosses no level shifter: the 1.8 V pre-drivers swing the gates of the pch33 steering switches between 0 and 1.8 V, which is enough because their sources sit at about 2.4 V (Q8).

**One reference sets the HS amplitude.** I = V(TX_VREF) / RREF through a 2/356 replica of the HS branch; XS copies it × 178 at the same drain voltage, so the amplitude is 17.8 mA × 22.5 Ω = 400 mV times the ratio of RREF to the line resistors (same poly), independent of the transistor parameters.

## 2. Sub-circuits

### 2.1 Bias `xc_bias` and UVLO `uvlo` (sheet `xc_bias.svg`)

| Device | Size | Role |
|---|---|---|
| RT, XT1, XO0 | 12 kΩ, 4/1 33 | tail of the OTA, about 200 µA from VDD33 (no start-up circuit needed) |
| XO1, XO2 / XO3, XO4 | 20/0.5 33 / 20/0.5 33 | OTA: NMOS pair (fb, TX_VREF), PMOS mirror, output vbp |
| XR | 2 × 10/1 33 | replica of 2 of the 356 units of XS |
| XRE, XRS | 3.4/0.35 33, 2.25/0.35 33, gates at 0 V | replica of XE (÷ 178) and of the on steering switch (÷ 178, n-well on its source like XSP) |
| RREF | 12 kΩ (poly, same type as the line resistors) | I = V(TX_VREF) / RREF = 100 µA; the TX_AMP trim goes here |
| XNB, XND | 10/1 33, 5/1 33 | 50 µA into the nch33 diode → nb |
| RU1, RU2, RUH, XUH | 290 kΩ, 200 kΩ, 10 kΩ | VDD33 divider; pok = 0 shorts RUH (hysteresis) |
| XU1..XU4, XUT | 4/1 33, tail 1/1 33 at nb (10 µA) | comparator tap vs TX_VREF; two inv33 → pok, pokb |

vbp carries the gate of XS (about 17 pF), which is the OTA's dominant pole: no compensation capacitor. The replica copies the whole branch, not only the source, so XS and XR see the same drain voltage when the line is at 0 … 0.4 V; the HS current varies by less than 0.05 % between DP = 0 and 0.8 V (section 4). The UVLO switches on above 1.2 V · 490 / 200 = 2.94 V and off below 1.2 V · 500 / 210 = 2.86 V (simulated 2.945 / 2.835 V).

### 2.2 HS output branch (sheet `xc_hs.svg`)

| Device | Size | Role |
|---|---|---|
| XS | 356 × 10/1 33, gate vbp | 17.8 mA current source |
| XE | 12 × 50/0.35 33, gate e33b | enable switch; opens with TX_EN & pok |
| XSP, XSN, XSD | 12 × 50/0.35 33, n-well on cs | steering to DP, DM and the dump; 12 units keep cs low enough (1.68 V at ss) for the 1.8 V gate drive to switch them off |
| RDQ | 22.5 Ω | dump load = the line (45 Ω ‖ 45 Ω), so cs does not move when the current changes path |
| CK | 1 pF, eb2 → vbp | turn-on kick cancellation |
| XKC, XKP / XKN / XKD | 10/0.35 33, 8/0.35 33, gate e33 | parking: pull cs and the three switch gates to VDD33 while TX is off |
| XTP, XTN, XTD | nch33 + pch33 12/0.35 TGs, gates e33 / e33b | cut the 1.8 V pre-drivers off the switch gates while parked |

**Turn-on kick.** When XE closes, the drain of XS falls from VDD33 out of triode by about 1.6 V and pulls about 3 pC out of vbp through its gate–drain capacitance: vbp dropped by 155 mV and the current overshot by about 60 % for more than 40 ns. CK couples the rising eb2 (TX_EN at 3.3 V, one inverter before e33b) into vbp with the same charge, so vbp is back within 5 mV 5 ns after TX_EN at every corner, well before the ready timer hands the current to the line. The cancellation depends on the drain swing, which depends on PVT; the first SYNC bit is within 1.2 % of the steady amplitude at all three corners (section 4.2). (The −11.6 % first SYNC bit of the first sizing at ss was not this kick but slow switching, see section 2.3.)

**Parking.** In FS / LS mode DP carries 3.3 V. If the steering switch gates sat at 1.8 V (pre-driver high), XSP would conduct from DP into cs, and the n-wells on cs would forward-bias. With TX_EN = 0 the TGs open and XKP / XKN / XKD pull the gates to VDD33, XKC pulls cs to VDD33: every junction on DP / DM is reverse biased, the line sees 0.1 nA.

### 2.3 Steering logic, pre-drivers and ready timer (sheet `xc_pre.svg`)

| Device | Size | Role |
|---|---|---|
| XI0..XI3 | core inverters | input buffers: pb, pq, nbr, nq |
| XGP, XGN | OAI21, 1 / 2 µm | XSP on = rdy & (p \| !n), XSN on = rdy & (n \| !p) |
| XBP0 / XBP1, XBN0 / XBN1, XBD0 / XBD1 | 1/2.5 inverter, then `sinv`: pfet 24 µm in series with XP2 = 18 × 5/0.36 at pbs (`msp`), nfet 6 µm | pre-drivers; `sinv` pulls a switch gate down at once (turn-on) and up at 18 × 50 µA (turn-off), which sets the line edge |
| XT0, XT1, XTS, CR | 1/0.18, nch33 1/2 at nb, 0.085 pF | ready timer: CR (0.085 pF) discharged by about 5 µA once ten = TX_EN & pok |
| XT2, XT3, XT4 | inverter, NAND2, inverter | rdy = ten & (cr below VDD18 / 2) |

TX_EN starts the current into the dump at once (XE closes, XSD is on while rdy = 0); about 16 ns later (14.1 / 16.0 / 17.3 ns at ss / tt / ff) rdy hands it to XSP / XSN: the line stays quiet for at least `t_en` / 2 = 10 ns (the checker), the source has settled, and the J of the lead is on the line well before SYNC starts 20.8 ns after TX_EN. With CR = 0.1 pF ready came at 20.0 ns at ff, only 0.8 ns before SYNC: the first SYNC edge started from a J of 148 mV and measured 495 ps (USB ≥ 500 ps). TX_EN falling clears rdy at once. P = N (TX_IN_P = TX_IN_N) opens XSP and XSN together: 8.9 mA each, the model's half / half. The OAI21 opens a switch one gate earlier than the other closes, so cs never floats (make before break); the turn-off ramp of `sinv` (18 × 50 µA from the reference into the switch gate) sets the tail of the line edge.

**Edge sizing over PVT.** A switch is off only when its gate is above cs − |Vth|, and the gates swing to VDD18 only. With the first sizing (8-unit switches, 12 µm series pfet, 16 × 50 µA) cs sat at 1.92 V at ss / 125 °C / 1.62 V: the starved pull-up ran out of headroom near VDD18, the old switch stayed half on for most of the bit, and alternating bits reached only 358 mV against 400 mV after a run (first SYNC bit −11.6 %, eye margin +7 mV). The ss failure looked like the turn-on kick, but vbp had settled within 5 ns and the dump carried the full current at ready: it was intersymbol interference. 12-unit switches (cs 1.68 V at ss), a 24 µm series pfet and 18 × 50 µA fix it (section 4.2). The larger switch gates also need stronger parking: with XKP / XKN / XKD at 4 µm, XKC pulled cs to VDD33 before the gates were up, a switch conducted from VDD33 into the line, and 11.5 mV was left on DP 5 ns after TX_EN fell at ss; 8 µm brings it to 3.5 mV. More pull-up current would also do it, but the ff edge would drop below 500 ps; wider pre-driver pull-downs or transmission gates make ss worse, because the TG capacitance slows the turn-off ramp.

### 2.4 FS / LS driver `fs_drv` (sheet `xc_fs.svg`), one per wire (XFP on DP, XFM on DM)

| Device | Size | Role |
|---|---|---|
| XMP, XMN | 14 × 40/0.35 33, 6 × 40/0.35 33 | push-pull output |
| RS | 26 Ω | series resistor; 33.5 / 34.6 Ω (high / low) in total (USB: 28 … 44 Ω) |
| CMP, CMN | 1 pF, from the output o to each gate | Miller capacitors: output slope = gate current / CM |
| XPO, XNO | 20/0.35 33, 10/0.35 33 | switch the output device off at once (break before make) |
| XPN + XPLS, XNP + XNLS | nch33 5/2 at nb, pch33 10/2 at vbp | LS gate currents |
| XPFS + XPFI, XNFS + XNFI | 7 × 5/1 at nb, 7 × 10/1 at vbp (`mfn`, `mfp`) | FS adds 7 × 50 µA |
| CGB | 0.5 pF on gn | balances the gate capacitance of XMN against XMP, so DP and DM start together (crossover in the middle) |

The FS logic (XF0..XF14, VDD18) makes u (pull-up on) and d (pull-down on) per wire: OE = 0 or !pok → both off; SE0 → both wires low; otherwise DAT ⊕ LS = 1 drives DP high (J at FS, K at LS) and DM low. Five `ls_up` shift u / d of both wires and the LS select to VDD33.

### 2.5 Receivers `se_rx`, `diff_rx` (sheet `xc_rx.svg`)

| Device | Size | Role |
|---|---|---|
| se_rx XP1/XP2, XN1/XN2 | pch33 / nch33 2.5/0.35 stacks | CMOS Schmitt trigger on VDD33 |
| se_rx XP3, XN3 | 0.8/0.35 33 | feedback: switching points 1.60 V (rising) and 1.26 V (falling) |
| diff_rx XP1, XP2 | 20/0.5 33, tail XPT 50 µA (vbp) | PMOS pair: works down to 0 V common mode |
| diff_rx XN1, XN2 | 10/0.5 33, tail XNT 50 µA (nb) | NMOS pair: works up to VDD33 |
| XN5, XN6 / XP5, XP6 | nch33 8/0.5 / pch33 16/0.5 diodes | loads of the two pairs |
| XN7, XP8 | gate d2p → d1n, gate d2n → d1p | sum the "a > b" currents of both pairs in XP5, the others in XN5 |
| XP7, XN8 | 16/0.5 33, 8/0.5 33 | output: up = I(XN1) + I(XP2), down = I(XP1) + I(XN2) |
| XHP*, XHN* | l = 4 µm sources, switched by the output | ±12 µA into the summing nodes: hysteresis 45 … 100 mV over the common mode |
| ls_dn | pch33 2 / nch33 1 inverter on VDD18 | 3.3 V → 1.8 V |

A single-pair comparator either lost its output swing at 0.4 V common mode (NMOS pair) or showed an offset at 2.5 V (PMOS pair). Summing both pairs keeps the gain from 0 V to VDD33. The outputs are gated in VDD18 logic: RXDP_A = sp & on, RXDM_A = sm & on, RXRCV_A = sd | !on, with on = FS_RX_EN_A & pok, the isolated values of spec 3.2.

### 2.6 Terminations and level shifters (sheet `xc_term.svg`)

| Device | Size | Role |
|---|---|---|
| XPU + RPU | pch33 60/0.35, 1.405 kΩ | DP pull-up 1.49 kΩ (USB idle: 0.9 … 1.575 kΩ; testbench: 1.425 … 1.575 kΩ) |
| XHP / XHM + RHP / RHM | nch33 13 × 40/0.35, 41.6 Ω | HS terminations 44.95 Ω at 0.2 … 0.4 V |

## 3. Pins

Same order and meaning as `afe_transceiver` in `afe_transceiver.va`: `TX_IN_P TX_IN_N TX_EN TX_I_5U TX_VREF TX_CAP_P TX_CAP_N FS_OE_A FS_DAT_A FS_SE0_A FS_LS_A FS_RX_EN_A RXRCV_A RXDP_A RXDM_A DP_PULLUP_EN_A HS_TERM_EN_A PD_EN_A0 PD_EN_A1 DP DM VDD33 VDD18 GND`. All logic inputs are 1.8 V levels, all outputs swing 0 … VDD18. TX_VREF (1.2 V) must be present: it sets every bias current (the model's `bias_mode` 1); TX_I_5U is not used (1 GΩ to GND), TX_CAP_P / TX_CAP_N connect to DP / DM through 1 TΩ (open, the model's default `r_cap`, Q10). Off (TX_EN = 0, FS_OE_A = 0, FS_RX_EN_A = 0, or VDD33 below about 2.84 V): no line current (0.1 nA), RXDP_A = RXDM_A = 0, RXRCV_A = 1.

## 4. Simulated results (ngspice)

`python3 AFE/spice/tb/tb_transceiver_tr.py` (ngspice 42, trapezoidal integration). The far end is a host model: 45 Ω terminations to ground in HS, 15 kΩ pull-downs in FS / LS. HS: 2 pF on each wire; FS: 50 pF; LS: 200 pF and 600 pF. Logic stimulus through 500 Ω with 50 ps edges.

Corners: tt 27 °C, 3.3 / 1.8 V; ss 125 °C, 3.0 / 1.62 V; ff −40 °C, 3.6 / 1.98 V (VDD33 / VDD18). **tt and ff pass. ss fails one check: the HS delay in `seq`, 1.341 ns against the testbench's corner window of 0.5 … 1.3 ns** (no USB requirement; kept as a failure, open point 8). Run on 2026-11-14 after the edge, ready-timer and parking changes of sections 2.2 and 2.3.

## 6. Open points

1. **Monte Carlo.** RXRCV_A offset (the summing mirrors and both pairs), the Schmitt points, the RREF / line resistor ratio (HS amplitude) and the CK kick cancellation are all mismatch-sensitive. With the xh018 mismatch models: check RXRCV_A within ±200 mV and the HS amplitude within ±10 % at 3σ; the TX_AMP and HS_TERM trims (spec 6) map onto RREF and RHP / RHM.
2. **Idle bias** of about 0.57 mA on VDD33 is always on. USB suspend allows 2.5 mA for the whole device, but an enable (from `usb_pwr_seq`, `afe_en`) that switches off the OTA, the receivers' tails and the vbp / nb references would bring it to nA; then the turn-on time of the bias needs a figure in the interface spec.
3. **Turn-on kick** is cancelled by a fixed 1 pF. The drain swing of XS depends on VDD33 and the line, so the cancellation is PVT dependent; it holds at the three corners (vbp settled within 5 ns, first SYNC bit within 1.2 %), but needs Monte Carlo. A dummy branch that keeps XS conducting into the dump while TX is off would remove the kick at the price of 17.8 mA.
4. **Released-line kick** (DM dips to −187 mV after FS_OE_A falls). Harmless for a receiver (SE0 either way), but it may show in a compliance capture; a slower switch-off of the gate (XPO / XNO weaker) or a clamp would remove it.
5. **ESD and back-power.** No ESD devices, no protection against DP / DM above VDD33 (VBUS short: 5.25 V) or against VDD33 = 0 with a powered line. The parking keeps every junction reverse-biased only while VDD33 is up.
6. **Trims:** TX_AMP (RREF), HS_TERM (RHP / RHM), FS slew (CM) are fixed here (spec 6 lists them).
7. **xh018 models:** replace `generic180.lib`, use the PDK resistors (poly for RREF / RDQ / RS / RHP / RHM) and capacitors (MIM for CK, CM, CR), rerun `tb_transceiver_tr.py` at all corners.
8. **HS delay and edge spread over PVT.** TX_IN → line 0.69 … 1.34 ns and 10–90 % edges 0.53 … 1.11 ns (ff … ss): the steering switches turn off only once their gates pass cs − |Vth| on a 1.8 V drive. The ss delay fails the testbench's corner window (1.3 ns, no USB limit; kept as a failure on 2026-11-14), and the ss crossing jitter (93 ps) is close to the testbench's 100 ps. The ff edges leave 33 ps to the USB minimum of 500 ps. Closing the spread needs a gate drive that tracks cs (pre-drivers on a supply that follows cs, or a bias that tracks |Vth| of pch33) rather than more sizing; the digital side absorbs the delay in the 10-bit lead.
