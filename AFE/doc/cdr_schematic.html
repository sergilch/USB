Open points

  • Coarse headroom at low VDD. At ss / 125 °C / 1.62 V the curve flattens at 489 MHz and K_fine halves. Widen XMPD / XMPM or shift current into XMOF, then recheck with the PLL's charge-pump range.
  • Dual-path charge pump. A second pump of about ICP / 8 straight into C2 brings the integral step down to about 260 ppm without a larger capacitor.
  • PLL VCO is the same cell. Identical V-to-I and ring with XMF on 0.9 V, placed beside this one; mismatch (risk R3) needs Monte Carlo with xh018 data.
  • xh018 models. Replace generic180.lib, map nch / pch in the two wrappers, use PDK R and C, retune RC / RF / XMOF, rerun lock at all corners.
  • Not yet characterised: charge-pump UP / DN mismatch over 0.6–1.2 V, jitter and supply sensitivity of the single-ended ring.
  • Spectre: AFE/veriloga/tb/tb_afe_cdr_tr.scs runs this netlist under the tb_afe_cdr checker; written, not run here.
