USB 2.0 PHY · AFE · line transceiver

afe_transceiver_tr schematic

Transistor-level design of everything that drives or loads DP / DM, as afe_transceiver.va models it: the HS current driver with replica bias, the FS / LS push-pull drivers with Miller slew control, the single-ended and differential receivers, and the switched pull-up, 45 Ω terminations and pull-downs. 180 nm: thick oxide on VDD33 for everything on the line, 1.8 V core logic on VDD18. Simulated with ngspice.

Open points

  • HS delay and edge spread over PVT: TX_IN → line 0.69 … 1.34 ns, edges 0.53 … 1.11 ns (ff … ss). The ss delay fails the testbench's 1.3 ns corner window (no USB limit; kept as a failure), ss crossing jitter is 93 of 100 ps, ff edges leave 33 ps to 500 ps. Closing the spread needs a gate drive that tracks cs, not more sizing.
  • Monte Carlo: the RXRCV_A offset, the Schmitt points, the RREF / line resistor ratio (HS amplitude) and the CK kick cancellation are mismatch sensitive. Check RXRCV_A within ±200 mV and the HS amplitude within ±10 % at 3σ; TX_AMP and HS_TERM trims map onto RREF and RHP / RHM.
  • Idle bias 0.5–0.7 mA on VDD33, always on. An enable from usb_pwr_seq (afe_en) would bring it to nA; the bias turn-on time then needs a figure in the interface spec.
  • Released-line kick (DM −187 mV after FS_OE_A falls): harmless for a receiver but visible in a compliance capture; a slower gate switch-off or a clamp removes it.
  • ESD and back-power: no ESD devices, no protection against DP / DM above VDD33 (VBUS short, 5.25 V) or against VDD33 = 0 with a powered line.
  • Trims TX_AMP (RREF), HS_TERM (RHP / RHM) and FS slew (CM) are fixed here.
  • xh018 models: replace generic180.lib, use PDK poly resistors and MIM capacitors, rerun all corners.
